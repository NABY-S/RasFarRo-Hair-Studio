What we do

Services

Four things done properly, plus the specials people ask for most.

Cuts

Haircut

Fades, tapers, line-ups and shaping for men, women and kids. Add a wash if you want to leave fresh.

Beard

Shaving

Clean edges and a neat beard, shaped to match your face and your cut.

Colour

Dye

Black dye to refresh, or colour dye for a bolder look. Ask for it with any cut.

Locks

Dreadlocks

Locks care with black dye and a wash, kept tidy and looking healthy.

Also on the chair: sakora, sporting waves, curls, perm cuts and coloured dye.
